Privacy Policy — Quedex

Last updated: 7 August 2026
Developer: Valerii Morhun ("we", "us")
Contact: valerii.morhun@gmail.com

Summary

Quedex is a single-player card game. It has no accounts, no login, no user profiles, no social features, and no advertising. We do not sell or share your personal data, and we do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

The game collects two limited categories of technical data: crash reports, so we can fix defects, and anonymous usage analytics, which you can turn off at any time in Settings. Neither category identifies you.

What the game stores on your device only

The following stays on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Saved games — deck state, discard pile, card order, and the name you give a save.
  • Settings — language, theme, sound and interface preferences, and your analytics choice.
  • Local statistics — play time, day streaks, and session, pass, and card counters.

Deleting the app removes this data. We cannot access it, restore it, or recover it for you.

Crash and error reports

To find and fix defects, the game sends crash and error reports through Sentry, an error monitoring service.

A crash report contains:

  • the error message and the technical stack trace,
  • the app version, build type, platform, and operating system version,
  • the technical context of the failure.

A crash report explicitly does not contain:

  • personal identifiers — the SDK's "default PII" collection is disabled,
  • screenshots, application logs, or a dump of the game's scene tree — all three attachments are disabled,
  • card text, save names, or filesystem paths — these are removed by a scrubbing step before any report leaves the device.

Legal basis (EEA/UK): our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and

functional (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Sentry acts as our processor. See the Sentry privacy policy.

Usage analytics — optional, off-switch in Settings

If analytics is enabled, the game sends anonymous, aggregated usage events through GameAnalytics so we can understand which packs and features are used and where players get stuck.

Legal basis (EEA/UK): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it

at any time in Settings → General → Privacy.

Every analytics event carries a fixed set of technical fields:

Events cover things like: session start and end, screen views, pack selection and progress, deck completion, language and theme changes, settings changes, save operations, purchase outcomes, and non-fatal errors.

To count returning players, the analytics provider stores a random anonymous identifier. It is generated by the provider layer, is not derived from any device identifier, and is deleted when you opt out.

What analytics never collects

We designed the event contract to exclude the following, and this exclusion is enforced in code:

  • your name, email address, or any account data — the game has no accounts,
  • device identifiers such as IDFA or Android ID,
  • your IP address (we do not log or store it), exact device model, or exact screen resolution,
  • the text of any card, question, or answer,
  • save names or filesystem paths,
  • payment details, receipts, or transaction identifiers,
  • precise location — we collect no location data of any kind.

When you turn analytics off

Turning analytics off in Settings takes effect immediately. The game stops collecting events, discards anything queued but not yet sent, deletes stored session state and retry state, and deletes the anonymous provider identifier. Crash reporting continues to operate, since it is covered by the separate legal basis described above.

Purchases

Card packs are sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see or receive your card number, billing address, or any payment credentials. Those stay between you and the platform, which processes them under its own privacy policy.

To unlock a pack and confirm the purchase back to the platform, the game receives a technical purchase confirmation from the store — the product identifier, a transaction or purchase identifier, and the purchase status. The same happens when you tap Restore purchases: the game asks the platform which products you already own.

Of that confirmation, only the product identifier is kept, in an encrypted file on your device. Transaction identifiers are used at the moment of verification and are neither stored by the game nor transmitted to us.

We operate no servers of our own, so no purchase data reaches us at all. Your entitlements live on your device and with the platform. This also means we cannot look up, restore, or refund a purchase for you — that is handled by the store under its own terms.

Purchase-related analytics events, when analytics is enabled, record only the outcome of a purchase — never its amount, receipt, or transaction identifier.

How we protect your information

We do not operate our own servers or user accounts, so there is no account database to breach. The technical data described above travels to our service providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and those providers maintain their own security programmes. Locally, the record of purchased packs is stored encrypted on your device.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs at one of our providers, we will act on their notification and inform affected users where the law requires it.

Data retention

  • Crash reports: retained by Sentry under its default retention period, then deleted automatically.
  • Analytics events: retained by GameAnalytics in aggregated form.
  • Data on your device: retained until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Children

Quedex is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to processing, or to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Because the game holds no account and no directly identifying data, we usually cannot link stored technical data to you as an individual. The most effective controls are in your hands:

  • Stop analytics collection: Settings → General → Privacy.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app.
  • Ask us: write to valerii.morhun@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and Virginia

These states grant additional rights: to know what categories of personal information are collected and for what purpose, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising.

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we run no advertising or targeted advertising of any kind. The only categories we handle are the technical identifiers and device characteristics listed above. To exercise any of these rights, or to appeal a decision on such a request, contact us at valerii.morhun@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved safeguard.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes affecting analytics will be reflected in the in-app privacy setting.
