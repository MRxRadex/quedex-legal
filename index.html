Summary

Quedex is a single-player card game with no accounts, login, social features, or advertising. We do not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or track players across other companies' apps or websites.

The game sends two limited categories of technical data: crash and error reports through Sentry, and pseudonymous usage analytics through GameAnalytics. Analytics is enabled by default and can be disabled at any time in Settings → General → Privacy.

"Pseudonymous" means the events do not contain a name, email address, account, or advertising ID, but they do carry random identifiers that let a provider recognize the same app installation or game session. Those identifiers and associated events are personal data under some privacy laws.

Data kept on your device

Saved games, save names, settings, and local play statistics are processed on your device and are not sent to us. Your analytics preference is also stored on the device.

While analytics is enabled, Quedex may temporarily keep an offline event queue, session recovery data, retry state, and a random GameAnalytics installation identifier on the device. Sentry maintains its own random installation identifier. Uninstalling the app removes this local data, subject to the operating system's backup and restore behavior.

Crash and error reports

Quedex uses Sentry to receive crash and error reports. A report may contain:

  • an error message, stack trace, and source location;
  • app version, build type, platform, and operating-system version;
  • technical context needed to reproduce the failure;
  • a random Sentry installation identifier; and
  • the IP address necessarily received by Sentry when the device connects to its service.

The game disables Sentry's default PII option, screenshots, log attachments, scene-tree attachments, automatic log breadcrumbs, and app-hang tracking. A scrubber removes save names and operating-system home-directory names from error messages and exception values before reports are sent. Quedex does not set a Sentry user name, email address, account identifier, purchase receipt, or transaction identifier.

Purpose and legal basis in the EEA/UK: diagnosing failures and maintaining the security, reliability, and functionality of the app, based on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR/UK GDPR).

Sentry acts as our service provider and processor. The Quedex Sentry project uses EU data residency. Crash reports are retained for the period configured in that project and then deleted; we set that period according to how long reports are reasonably needed to detect and diagnose recurring defects. See Sentry's privacy notice.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics is enabled by default. When enabled, Quedex sends events to GameAnalytics. Events describe actions such as launches and sessions, screen views, pack selection and coarse progression, deck completion, language and theme changes, settings categories used, save operations, purchase outcomes, and grouped non-fatal errors.

Events and required provider annotations may include:

  • a random persistent GameAnalytics installation identifier;
  • a random identifier for each game session and its sequential session number;
  • event time and time since the session began;
  • app, build, game-engine, and analytics-client versions;
  • platform, major operating-system version, and manufacturer (Apple on Apple platforms and unknown elsewhere);
  • UI language, a coarse memory-based device tier, and a coarse screen name;
  • pack/product identifiers and bucketed or aggregated gameplay measurements;
  • the device's network IP address as connection data; GameAnalytics documents that it anonymizes the stored IP and uses it to infer geographic fields such as country and, where available, region or city.

The app does not send GameAnalytics your name, email address, account data, IDFA, Android Advertising ID, Android ID, exact device model, exact screen resolution, precise GPS location, card/question/answer text, save names, filesystem paths, payment details, receipts, or store transaction identifiers.

Purpose and legal basis in the EEA/UK: understanding how the game is used, improving progression and features, and monitoring performance, based on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR/UK GDPR). You can object to this processing at any time by disabling analytics in Settings → General → Privacy.

GameAnalytics acts as our service provider and processor. Its published retention schedule is: raw and annotated data for one month, Player Warehouse data for one year, and annotated Player Lookup data used for retention calculations for 18 months. See the GameAnalytics Developer Policy and Data Processing Addendum.

When you turn analytics off

Turning analytics off takes effect immediately for future analytics events. Quedex discards its unsent queue and removes its local analytics session, retry, and GameAnalytics identifier files. It also restarts the Sentry runtime scope to clear buffered analytics breadcrumbs; crash reporting remains active.

Turning analytics off does not automatically erase events that were already delivered to GameAnalytics. You may contact us to exercise any deletion or other legal right that applies to those records. Because Quedex has no account and the identifiers are random, we may be unable to associate provider records with you unless enough technical information is available to verify the request.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments under its own privacy terms. Quedex does not receive your card number, billing address, or payment credentials.

The game receives a product identifier, transaction or purchase identifier, and purchase status to verify a purchase or restore an entitlement. Only the product identifier is retained in the encrypted local entitlement file. Transaction identifiers are used for verification and are not retained by the game or sent to us.

When analytics is enabled, purchase analytics contains the static product identifier and a grouped outcome. It does not contain the price, payment details, receipt, or transaction identifier. Store consoles remain the source of authoritative purchase and revenue records.

Security and international transfers

Technical data is sent over encrypted network connections. Sentry and GameAnalytics apply their own administrative, technical, and physical security measures. No system is completely secure.

Providers may process data outside your country. The Quedex Sentry project uses EU data residency. GameAnalytics states that relevant transfers to its US infrastructure and subprocessors rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Its DPA lists AWS, GCP, and Imply as subprocessors for such transfers.

Children

Quedex is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect a child's name, contact details, account information, or content. The technical identifiers used for analytics and crash reporting may nevertheless be treated as personal information under children's privacy laws.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data through Quedex, contact us. We will review the request and delete data where we can identify it and the law requires deletion. Analytics can also be disabled in the game's privacy settings.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, appeal a decision, or complain to a data-protection authority.

Quedex has no user account and we deliberately avoid direct identifiers. This limits our ability to find a particular person's pseudonymous provider record, but it does not remove applicable rights. To make a request, email valerii.morhun@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to verify and locate the relevant record and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

US state privacy laws may also provide rights concerning access, correction, deletion, portability, and opt-out from certain sales, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling. We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising or qualifying profiling. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.
